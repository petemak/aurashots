About Aura Shots

Not just photos…it's an aura.

Story and style

Aura Shots is a private photography studio in Kanombe, Kigali. Our work is editorial and cinematic: we build each frame around colour, dramatic light and the kind of main-character energy that makes you stop scrolling.

We photograph locals, working professionals and visitors who want pictures that feel like a magazine, not a studio snapshot.

How booking works

  1. Choose a package and add it to your session basket.
  2. Pick your preferred date and time and tell us where to reach you.
  3. We confirm by email or WhatsApp, or suggest another time that works.
  4. Pay by cash or mobile money after confirmation, then enjoy your session.

On the day, bring your outfits and your energy. We guide your poses and the light.

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept walk-ins?

No. Aura Shots is by appointment only, so every session starts with a request on this website.

Can I get a refund?

No. Bookings are commitments and we have a strict no-refund policy. Please read the refund policy before you book.

How do I pay?

You pay by cash or mobile money after we confirm your session. Nothing is charged on the website.

How long until I receive my pictures?

Each package lists its delivery time under What's included on its page.

I am visiting Rwanda. Can I book?

Yes. Choose a package, pick a date and add your WhatsApp number so we can agree the meeting place with you.

Can I change my date?

Message us on WhatsApp or by email as early as you can and we will try to find another time.

How does merchandise delivery work?

Orders are requests until we approve them. After approval you pay by cash or mobile money and we deliver within Rwanda. The delivery fee is confirmed when we approve.
