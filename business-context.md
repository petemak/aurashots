# Business Context

## What we do

[Aura Shots is a private photography service and brand based in Kigali, Rwanda. Rather than a public studio or a traditional camera shop, we operate as an exclusive, 
 appointment-only creative photography business. 

- The Concept: We operate under the tagline "Not just photos…it's an aura," and focuses on stylized portraiture, lifestyle photography, and birthday shoots.
- Booking Structure: We do not accept walk-ins. Clients must book sessions in advance by sending a direct message (DM) through our
  social media profile on Instagram (@aura_shxts) or TikTok.
- Policy: All bookings require a commitment since we invest time and effort in preparing and setting up sessions. We strictly enforce a no-refund policy.


Currently, customers looking to book a session, normally check out our social media portfolio to see our exact style of photography and inquire directly 
about our current rates and packages.

That outreach is not sufficient. We want to reach out to a different customer group and that is urban professionals and international tourists. What we want 
is a professional web shop where we, as shop owners, can publish services and reach out to potential clients that don’t use social media.

The shop should feature the following:
1) The Service Catalogue which lists all avalibale service and packages with a short description. 
2) A photo Portfolio containing a curated collection of photos to showcase what the services.

For the business owner (root account) shout provide the following functionality:

- Sign in/out
- Add/remove support employee account.
- Manage the photography service catalogue (packages, short descriptio, detailed descriptions, prices, contents, conditions)
- Add/remove a service package (name, description, price, contents, images) 
- View booking/orders
- Manage Portfolio (add/remove/update pictures and descriptions)
- Manage calendar (block or unblock dates)
- Process bookings and confirm schedules

For appointed support personnel:
- sign in/out
- Manage the photography service catalogue (packages, descriptions, prices (RwF), contents, conditions)
- Add/remove a service package (name, description, price, contents, images) 
- View booking/sorders
- Manage Portfolio (add/remove/update pictures and descriptions)
- Manage calendar (block or unblock dates\}
- Process bookings and confirm schedules

Customer:
- Browse service catalogue
- Select a service and view details description (name, description, price, contents, pictures) 
- Add service to shopping cart
- View shopping cart
- Enter name, phone, email, address, select a date and submit request
- Expect response via mail or WhatsApp

Further rules:
- A service must have a price before it can be made available for ordering online.
- Merchandise stock is not counted: if in stock then a label is displayed next to the product. and a product with no stock shows as sold out.
- Staff cannot enter bookings on behalf of customers. All bookings are placed by customers through the webshop.
- Policy texts (terms of sale, privacy, returns, order terms) are drafted as placeholders and reviewed by a human before launch.]
