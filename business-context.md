# Business Context

## What we do

[Aura Shots is a private photography service and brand based in Kigali, Rwanda. Rather than a public studio or a traditional camera shop, we operate as an exclusive, appointment-only creative photography business. 

- The Concept: We operate under the tagline "Not just photos…it's an aura," and focuses on stylized portraiture, lifestyle photography, and birthday shoots.

- Booking Structure: We do not accept walk-ins. Clients must book sessions in advance by sending a direct message (DM) through our social media profile on Instagram (@aura\_shots) or TikTok.

- Policy: All bookings require a commitment since we invest time and effort in preparing and setting up sessions. We strictly enforce a no-refund policy.

Our service packages and prices:

1. Single Session – 50000 RwF

2. Couple Session – 100000 RwF

3. Besties-Package  - 12000 RwF

4. Special Package – 100000 RwF

5. Weekend Sessions – 15000 RwF


we also intend to sell merchandise and lif-style items that relate to photography:

- Clothing (T-Shirts, hoodies, ..)

- Prints and framed art

 

For all these services we define package contents:

- Number of edited pictures

- Number of videos

- Outfits to wear

- Delivery time

- Locations

- Related merchandise like picture frames, 

Currently, customers looking to book a session, normally check out our social media portfolio to see our exact style of photography and inquire directly about our current rates and packages.

That outreach is not sufficient. We want to reach out to a different customer group and that is urban professionals and international tourists. What we want is a professional web shop where we, as shop owners, can publish services and reach out to potential clients that don’t use social media.

For the business owner (root account) shout provide the following functionality:

- Sign in/out

- Add/remove support employee account.

- Manage the photography service catalogue (packages, descriptions, prices, contents, conditions)

- Add/remove a service package (name, description, price, contents, images) 

- View booking/sorders

- Manage calendar (block or unblock dates\}

- Process bookings and confirm schedules


For appointed support personnel:

- sign in/out

- Manage the photography service catalogue (packages, descriptions, prices, contents, conditions)

- Add/remove a service package (name, description, price, contents, images) 

- View booking/sorders

- Manage calendar (block or unblock dates\}

- Process bookings and confirm schedules

Customer:

- Browse service catalogue

- Select a service and view details description (name, description, price, contents, pictures) 

- Add service to shopping cart

- View shopping cart

- Enter name, phone, email, address, select a date and submit request

- Expect response via mail or WhatsApp

Customers access webshop and order as guests. Payment in cash or mobile money after approval and discussions with the business. The business owner or support personnel contact the client.

Further rules:

- A service must have a price before it can be made available for ordering online.

- Merchandise stock is not counted: if in stock then a label is displayed next to the product. and a product with no stock shows as sold out.

- Staff cannot enter bookings on behalf of customers. All bookings are placed by customers through the webshop.

- Policy texts (terms of sale, privacy, returns, order terms) are drafted as placeholders and reviewed by a human before launch.]
