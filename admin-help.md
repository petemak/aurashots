# Aura Shots Admin Help

## getting-started | Finding your way around | roles: owner, support | pages: A3 | group: Getting started
A quick tour of the Dashboard and what to do first each day.

When you sign in you arrive at the **Dashboard**. It shows what is waiting for you:

- **Session requests waiting for you**: customers who asked for a photo session.
- **Merchandise orders waiting for you**: customers who asked to buy clothing, prints or framed art.
- **Messages to answer**: questions sent from the Contact page.
- **Coming up**: the next confirmed sessions.

Click any card to open that list. The menu on the left (or at the top on a phone) takes you to every area you are allowed to use. Every page has a **How do I…?** link that opens the help for that page.

> Customers place all bookings themselves on the website. Staff cannot enter a booking for a customer. If someone messages you on Instagram, ask them to use the website.

Next: [Review a session request](help:review-session-request), [Process a merchandise order](help:process-order).

## sign-in | Signing in and out | roles: owner, support | pages: A1, A2 | group: Getting started
How to sign in, sign out, and what to do if you forget your password.

1. Open the admin address and go to the **Sign in** page.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**. You arrive at the Dashboard.
3. To leave, open **My account** and click **Sign out**.

### I forgot my password
1. On the **Sign in** page click **Forgotten your password?**
2. Type your email and click **Send me a reset link**. You will always see the same message, whether or not the email is known.
3. Open the email and click the link. It works once and lasts 60 minutes.
4. Type a new password twice (at least 12 characters) and click **Save password**. Then sign in.

> After five wrong attempts you must wait a few minutes before trying again.

## change-password | Changing your password | roles: owner, support | pages: A21 | group: Getting started
Change your own password or check your account details.

1. Click **My account** in the menu. You see your name, email and role.
2. Under **Change password**, type your **Current password**, then the **New password** twice.
3. Click **Save password**. You see a green "Saved" message.

> Changing your password signs you out on other devices.

## review-session-request | Reviewing a session request | roles: owner, support | pages: A4, A5 | group: Session bookings
Open a new request, check the details and talk to the customer.

1. Click **Session requests** in the menu. The list opens on **Waiting for you**.
2. Click a request. The **Session request** page opens with the customer, what they asked for, the date and time they prefer, and any notes.
3. Check the **Agreement accepted** box. It shows who agreed, when, and which version of the booking agreement. You cannot change it.
4. If there is a yellow warning on the date and time, that slot is blocked or already taken by another confirmed session.
5. To talk to the customer, click **Message on WhatsApp** (it opens WhatsApp with a ready message) or use their email. Agree wishes, location and price.
6. Write anything the team should remember under **Internal notes** and click **Add note**. Customers never see notes.
7. Now [confirm the session](help:confirm-session) or [decline the request](help:decline-session).

> A waiting request does not block the calendar. Two customers can ask for the same time. Only a confirmed session blocks it.

## confirm-session | Confirming or changing a session date and time | roles: owner, support | pages: A5 | group: Session bookings
Confirm a request, keep or change the time, and tell the customer.

1. Open the request from **Session requests**.
2. Click **Confirm this date and time**. A small form appears with the customer's preferred date and time already filled in.
3. Change the **Date** or **Start time** if needed, and type a **Note to the customer** (for example the meeting place).
4. Click **Confirm**. The status changes to **Confirmed**.
5. The customer is emailed straight away. Under **Messages sent** you can see if it went. If it shows a problem, click **Send again**. You can also send the same news by WhatsApp using **Message on WhatsApp**.

### Moving a confirmed session
1. Open the confirmed request and click **Change date or time**.
2. Choose the new date and time, add a note, and click **Confirm**. The customer is emailed.

> The new time is hidden from other customers on the booking calendar as soon as you confirm it.
>
> If the time clashes with a block or another session, you see a warning. Choose a different time unless you really mean to double up.

## decline-session | Declining a session request | roles: owner, support | pages: A5 | group: Session bookings
Say no to a request and give the customer a reason.

1. Open the request and click **Decline request**.
2. Type the **Reason** in plain, polite words. The customer will read it.
3. A box asks "Are you sure?". Click **Yes, decline**. The status changes to **Declined** and the customer is emailed.

> A declined request cannot be reopened. If you change your mind, ask the customer to submit a new request.

## session-payment-complete | Recording payment and finishing a session | roles: owner, support | pages: A5 | group: Session bookings
Note that the customer has paid and close the booking after the shoot.

1. Open the confirmed request.
2. After you receive the money, click **Mark as paid**. Choose **Cash** or **Mobile money**, check the **Amount (RwF)**, and click **Save**.
3. After the shoot, click **Mark as completed**.

> Payment is taken in person or by mobile money after confirmation. Nothing is charged on the website.
>
> Aura Shots has a strict no-refund policy. The system does not refund anything.

## process-order | Processing a merchandise order | roles: owner, support | pages: A6, A7 | group: Merchandise orders
Approve or reject an order, record payment and mark it delivered.

1. Click **Merchandise orders** in the menu. Open an order under **Waiting for you**.
2. Check the items, the delivery address, and the **Agreement accepted** box.
3. Contact the customer if anything is unclear, using **Message on WhatsApp** or email.
4. To accept, click **Approve order**. If the box **Delivery fee (RwF)** is empty, type the fee first. Add a note for the customer if you like, then click **Approve**. The customer is emailed.
5. To refuse, click **Reject order**, type a **Reason**, and confirm. The customer is emailed.
6. When the customer has paid in cash or by mobile money, click **Mark as paid** and choose the method.
7. When the goods have been handed over, click **Mark as delivered**.

> Stock is not counted. Before you approve, check you really have the item. If you do not, reject the order and say so in the reason.
>
> Rejected and delivered orders are final.

## add-service | Adding a photography package | roles: owner, support | pages: A8, A9 | group: Services
Create a new package customers can book.

1. Click **Services** in the menu, then **Add a service**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Short description** (shown on cards) and **Detailed description**.
3. Type the **Price (RwF)**. A service cannot be published without a price.
4. Under **What's included** fill in **Number of edited pictures**, **Number of videos**, **Outfits**, **Delivery time** and **Locations**.
5. Write the **Conditions**. Optionally pick **Related merchandise**.
6. Click **Add pictures**, choose files, and write a short **Picture description** for each one. Drag to reorder; the first picture is the main one.
7. Set **Status** to **Published** to show it on the website, or **Hidden** to keep it private.
8. Click **Save**. You see "Saved". Click **View on the website** to check it.

> Changes appear on the website within about a minute.

## change-service-price | Changing a price or editing a package | roles: owner, support | pages: A8, A9 | group: Services
Edit a package, including its price, contents or pictures.

1. Click **Services** and then **Edit** on the package.
2. Change the **Price (RwF)** or any other field.
3. Click **Save**.

To hide a package for a while, set **Status** to **Hidden** and save.

> A new price applies only to new requests. Requests already received keep the price the customer saw.
>
> A package marked **Needs a price** cannot be published until you type a price.

## remove-service | Removing a package | roles: owner, support | pages: A9 | group: Services
Take a package off the website for good.

1. Open the package from **Services**.
2. Scroll down and click **Remove this service**.
3. A box asks "Are you sure? This cannot be undone". Click **Yes, remove**.

> The package disappears from the website. Old booking requests still show it. If you only want a pause, choose **Hidden** instead.

## add-merchandise | Adding or editing a merchandise item | roles: owner, support | pages: A10, A11 | group: Merchandise
Add clothing, prints or framed art and set its sizes or colours.

1. Click **Merchandise**, then **Add an item** (or **Edit** on an existing one).
2. Choose the **Category**, type the **Name**, **Description**, **Details** and **Price (RwF)**.
3. Under **Options** click **Add an option**, for example Size: M, or Colour: Black. Keep **In stock** ticked for the ones you have.
4. Add pictures with a short description for each.
5. Tick **New** to show the New label. Choose **Published** or **Hidden**.
6. Click **Save**.

> The website does not count stock. It only shows the **In stock** or **Sold out** label you set.

## mark-sold-out | Marking an item sold out or back in stock | roles: owner, support | pages: A10, A11 | group: Merchandise
Quickly change what customers can order.

1. Click **Merchandise**.
2. On the item's card click **Mark as sold out** (or **Mark as in stock**). The label changes at once.
3. To change only one size or colour, click **Edit** and untick **In stock** next to that option, then **Save**.

> Customers cannot add sold-out items to their bag. Orders already received are not affected.

## remove-merchandise | Removing a merchandise item | roles: owner, support | pages: A11 | group: Merchandise
Delete an item from the shop.

1. Open the item and scroll to the bottom.
2. Click **Remove this item**, then **Yes, remove** in the box that asks "Are you sure? This cannot be undone".

> Old orders still show the item. To hide it for a while, choose **Hidden** instead.

## manage-portfolio | Managing the portfolio | roles: owner, support | pages: A12, A13 | group: Portfolio
Add, edit, reorder or remove photos in the showcase.

### Add a photo
1. Click **Portfolio**, then **Add a photo**.
2. Click **Choose a photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP). A preview appears.
3. Type a **Caption**, a **Description for screen readers** (say what the picture shows, in a sentence) and choose a **Category**.
4. Tick **Show on home page** for your best work.
5. Click **Save**.

### Edit or reorder
1. On the **Portfolio** page click a photo, change the details and click **Save**.
2. To reorder, use the **Move up** and **Move down** buttons under a photo.

### Remove a photo
1. Open the photo and click **Remove this photo**.
2. Click **Yes, remove** when asked.

> The description for screen readers is required. It helps blind visitors and search engines.

## block-dates | Blocking and unblocking dates and times | roles: owner, support | pages: A14 | group: Calendar
Stop customers choosing days or hours when you are not available.

1. Click **Calendar**. Blocked days are red, confirmed sessions are pink.
2. Click a day. A panel opens showing that day.
3. To block the whole day, click **Block this whole day**, add a **Reason**, and click **Block**.
4. To block part of the day, click **Block certain hours**, choose **From** and **To**, and click **Block**.
5. To block several days (for a holiday), use **Block several days**, choose **From date** and **To date**, and click **Block**.
6. To open a day again, click **Unblock** next to the block and confirm.

> Customers cannot choose blocked days or hours on the booking page.
>
> Blocking does not cancel sessions that are already confirmed. If there are any, the page lists them so you can contact those customers.

## answer-enquiry | Answering a message from the Contact page | roles: owner, support | pages: A15 | group: Messages
Read a question and reply.

1. Click **Messages**. New ones are marked **Waiting**.
2. Click a message to read it.
3. Click **Reply by email** or **Message on WhatsApp** if the customer left a phone number.
4. After you reply, click **Mark as answered**.

> Customers expect a reply by email or WhatsApp. If they ask about a booking, point them to the booking page.

## site-settings | Updating contact details, opening hours and delivery areas | roles: owner, support | pages: A16 | group: Settings
Fill in the details marked TBD and keep the rest up to date.

1. Click **Settings**. Each group has its own **Save** button.
2. **Contact and social**: update phone, WhatsApp, email, address and the **Instagram**, **TikTok** and **Facebook** links. Anything left empty is hidden from visitors.
3. **Opening hours**: change the hours shown and used by the booking calendar.
4. **Booking rules**: set the default session length, the minimum notice and how far ahead customers can book.
5. **Delivery areas and fees**: click **Add an area**, type the **Area name** and **Fee (RwF)**, and keep **Delivers here** ticked. To stop delivering somewhere, untick it, or click **Remove** and confirm.
6. Click **Save** in that group.

> If you leave a delivery fee empty, customers see "to be confirmed" and you type the fee when you approve their order.

## add-staff | Adding a staff member | roles: owner | pages: A17, A18 | group: Team
Give a team member access.

1. Click **Staff accounts**, then **Add a staff member**.
2. Type their **Name** and **Email**.
3. Click **Send invitation**. They get an email with a link to choose a password.

> Staff accounts are support staff. They can manage bookings, orders, services, merchandise, portfolio, calendar, messages and settings. They cannot manage staff or edit policies.

## manage-staff | Resetting a password, switching off or removing a staff member | roles: owner | pages: A19 | group: Team
Look after an existing staff account.

### Reset a password
1. Open **Staff accounts** and click the person.
2. Click **Send a password reset link**. They receive an email.

### Switch off access
1. Click **Switch off access**. They are signed out at once and cannot sign in.
2. To allow them again, click **Switch on access**.

### Remove a staff member
1. Click **Remove this staff member**.
2. Click **Yes, remove** in the box that asks "Are you sure? This cannot be undone".

> Their past actions stay in the records under their name. You cannot remove your own account.

## edit-policies | Editing policies and the customer agreements | roles: owner | pages: A20 | group: Team
Change the legal texts and the agreements customers accept when they order.

1. Click **Policies and agreements**. Documents marked **Draft – needs review** must be checked by you before the website goes live.
2. Click a document, then edit the text.
3. Write a short **What changed** note.
4. Click **Publish as new version**. Confirm when asked.
5. When you are happy with a draft, click **Mark as reviewed**.

> Customers must accept the newest version when they book or order. Earlier acceptances stay on record with the exact version each customer agreed to. You cannot change or delete a past acceptance.
>
> The refund policy states that there are no refunds. Please have a professional read all texts before launch.
