# Aura Shots Admin Help

## getting-started | Finding your way around | roles: owner, support | pages: A3 | group: Getting started
A quick tour of the Dashboard and what to do first each day.

When you sign in you arrive at the **Dashboard**. It shows what is waiting for you:

- **Session requests waiting for you**: customers who asked for a photo session.
- **Merchandise orders waiting for you**: customers who asked to buy clothing, prints or framed art.
- **Messages to answer**: questions sent from the Contact page.
- **Coming up: confirmed sessions**: the next confirmed sessions.

Click any card to open that list. Under **Shortcuts** you can jump straight to **Add a service**, **Add a photo** or **Block a date**. The menu on the left (or behind the menu button on a phone) takes you to every area you are allowed to use. Every page has a **How do I…?** link that opens the help for that page.

> Customers place all bookings themselves on the website. Staff cannot enter a booking for a customer. If someone messages you on Instagram, ask them to use the website.

Next: [Review a session request](help:review-session-request), [Process a merchandise order](help:process-order).

## sign-in | Signing in and out | roles: owner, support | pages: A1, A2 | group: Getting started
How to sign in, sign out, and what to do if you forget your password.

1. Open the admin address and go to the **Sign in** page.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**. You arrive at the Dashboard.
3. To leave, open **My account** and click **Sign out**.

### I forgot my password
1. On the **Sign in** page click **Forgotten your password?**
2. Type your email and click **Send me a reset link**. You will always see the same message, whether or not the email is known.
3. Open the email and click the link. It works once and lasts 60 minutes.
4. Type a new password in **New password** and again in **Repeat the new password** (at least 12 characters) and click **Save password**. You are taken to the **Sign in** page. Sign in with the new password.

> After five wrong attempts you must wait a few minutes before trying again.
>
> If no email arrives, the shop's email may not be set up yet. Ask the owner for a new sign-in link.

## change-password | Changing your password | roles: owner, support | pages: A21 | group: Getting started
Change your own password or check your account details.

1. Click **My account** in the menu. Under **Your details** you see your name, email and role.
2. Under **Change password**, type your **Current password**, then the **New password** and **Repeat the new password**.
3. Click **Save password**. A green "Saved" message appears.

> Changing your password signs you out on other devices.

## review-session-request | Reviewing a session request | roles: owner, support | pages: A4, A5 | group: Session bookings
Open a new request, check the details and talk to the customer.

1. Click **Session requests** in the menu. The list opens on **Waiting for you**. Use **Confirmed**, **Declined**, **Completed** or **All** to see other requests.
2. Click a request. The **Session request** page opens with the **Customer**, **What they asked for** (services, total, the date and time they asked for, and their notes) and the payment status.
3. Look at the **Agreement accepted** section. It shows who agreed, when, and which version of the booking agreement. Click **View the exact text they accepted** to read it. You cannot change it.
4. If there is a yellow warning above the date and time, that time is blocked on your calendar or already used by another confirmed session.
5. To talk to the customer, click **Message on WhatsApp** (it opens WhatsApp with a ready message) or use their email address. Agree wishes, location and price.
6. Write anything the team should remember in the **Add a note** box under **Internal notes** and click **Add note**. Customers never see notes.
7. Now [confirm the session](help:confirm-session) or [decline the request](help:decline-session). The buttons are in the **Decision** section.

> A waiting request does not block the calendar. Two customers can ask for the same time. Only a confirmed session blocks it.

## confirm-session | Confirming or changing a session date and time | roles: owner, support | pages: A5 | group: Session bookings
Confirm a request, keep or change the time, and tell the customer.

1. Open the request from **Session requests**.
2. In the **Decision** section click **Confirm this date and time**. A small form appears with the customer's preferred date and time already filled in.
3. Change the **Date** or **Start time** if needed, and type a **Note to the customer** (for example the meeting place).
4. Click **Confirm**. The status changes to **Confirmed** and a green message tells you the customer will be emailed.
5. Under **Messages sent** you can see if the email went. It says **Sent**, **Not sent yet** or **Could not be sent**. If it is not sent, click **Send again**. You can also send the same news by WhatsApp using **Message on WhatsApp**.

### Moving a confirmed session
1. Open the confirmed request and click **Change date or time**.
2. Choose the new **Date** and **Start time**, add a note, and click **Confirm**. The customer is emailed.

> The new time is hidden from other customers on the booking calendar as soon as you confirm it.
>
> If the time clashes with a block or another session, the form tells you so. Choose a different time. Only click **Confirm anyway (double up)** if you really mean to have two sessions at once.

## decline-session | Declining a session request | roles: owner, support | pages: A5 | group: Session bookings
Say no to a request and give the customer a reason.

1. Open the request and click **Decline request** in the **Decision** section. A form opens.
2. Type the **Reason** in plain, polite words. The customer will read it.
3. Click the red **Decline request** button in the form.
4. A box asks "Are you sure?". Click **Yes, decline** (or **Cancel** to go back). The status changes to **Declined** and the customer is emailed.

> A declined request cannot be reopened. If you change your mind, ask the customer to submit a new request.

## session-payment-complete | Recording payment and finishing a session | roles: owner, support | pages: A5 | group: Session bookings
Note that the customer has paid and close the booking after the shoot.

1. Open the confirmed request.
2. After you receive the money, click **Mark as paid**. Under **How did they pay?** choose **Cash** or **Mobile money**, check the **Amount (RwF)**, and click **Save**.
3. After the shoot, click **Mark as completed**.

> Payment is taken in person or by mobile money after confirmation. Nothing is charged on the website.
>
> Aura Shots has a strict no-refund policy. The system does not refund anything.

## process-order | Processing a merchandise order | roles: owner, support | pages: A6, A7 | group: Merchandise orders
Approve or reject an order, record payment and mark it delivered.

1. Click **Merchandise orders** in the menu. Open an order under **Waiting for you**. The other tabs are **Approved**, **Paid**, **Delivered**, **Rejected** and **All**.
2. Check the **Items**, the **Delivery** details, and the **Agreement accepted** section.
3. Contact the customer if anything is unclear, using **Message on WhatsApp** or their email address.
4. To accept, click **Approve order**. In the form, if **Delivery fee (RwF)** is empty, type the fee first. Add a **Note to the customer** if you like, then click **Approve**. The customer is emailed.
5. To refuse, click **Reject order**, type a **Reason**, and click **Reject order** in the form. A box asks "Are you sure?". Click **Yes, reject**. The customer is emailed.
6. When the customer has paid in cash or by mobile money, click **Mark as paid**, choose **Cash** or **Mobile money**, check the **Amount (RwF)** and click **Save**.
7. When the goods have been handed over, click **Mark as delivered**.
8. If the customer pays when you hand over the goods, click **Mark as delivered (paid on delivery)** instead. Choose how they paid, check **Amount received (RwF)** and click **Save**. This records the payment and the delivery together.

> Stock is not counted. Before you approve, check you really have the item. If you do not, reject the order and say so in the reason.
>
> Rejected and delivered orders are final.

## add-service | Adding a photography package | roles: owner, support | pages: A8, A9 | group: Services
Create a new package customers can book.

1. Click **Services** in the menu, then **Add a service**.
2. Under **About the service** fill in **Name**, **Short description** (shown on cards) and **Detailed description**.
3. Type the **Price (RwF)**. A service cannot be published without a price. Fill in **Session length (minutes)** only if this package is longer or shorter than the standard length in Settings.
4. Under **What's included** fill in **Number of edited pictures**, **Number of videos**, **Outfits**, **Delivery time** and **Locations**.
5. Write the **Conditions**. Optionally tick items under **Related merchandise (optional)**.
6. Under **Pictures** click **Add pictures**, choose files, and write a short **Picture description** for each one. Use the **Move up** and **Move down** buttons to change the order; the first picture is the main one. Click **Take out** to remove a picture from the package.
7. Under **Visibility**, tick **Show on the home page** if you want it featured. Set **Status** to **Published** to show it on the website, or **Hidden** to keep it private.
8. Click **Save**. A green message says "Saved." Click **View on the website (opens in a new tab)** to check it.

> Changes appear on the website within about a minute.

## change-service-price | Changing a price or editing a package | roles: owner, support | pages: A8, A9 | group: Services
Edit a package, including its price, contents or pictures.

1. Click **Services** and then **Edit** on the package.
2. Change the **Price (RwF)** or any other field.
3. Click **Save**.

To hide a package for a while, click **Hide from shop** on its card in the **Services** list (or set **Status** to **Hidden** and save). To show it again, click **Show on shop**.

To change the order of the packages, use **Move up** and **Move down** on the cards in the **Services** list.

> A new price applies only to new requests. Requests already received keep the price the customer saw.
>
> A package marked **Needs a price** cannot be published until you type a price.

## remove-service | Removing a package | roles: owner, support | pages: A9 | group: Services
Take a package off the website for good.

1. Open the package from **Services**.
2. Scroll down to the **Remove** section and click **Remove this service**.
3. A box asks "Are you sure? This cannot be undone." Click **Yes, remove**.

> The package disappears from the website. Old booking requests still show it. If you only want a pause, choose **Hidden** instead.

## add-merchandise | Adding or editing a merchandise item | roles: owner, support | pages: A10, A11 | group: Merchandise
Add clothing, prints or framed art and set its sizes or colours.

1. Click **Merchandise**, then **Add an item** (or **Edit** on an existing one).
2. Choose the **Category** (**Clothing**, **Prints** or **Framed art**), type the **Name**, **Description**, **Details** and **Price (RwF)**.
3. Under **Options** click **Add an option group** and type its name in **Option group (for example Size or Colour)**. Fill in the first **Option**, for example M or Black. Click **Add an option** for more. Keep **In stock** ticked for the ones you have. Use **Take out option** or **Take out this group** to remove one.
4. Under **Pictures** click **Add pictures** and write a **Picture description** for each one. Use **Move up** and **Move down** to change the order.
5. Under **Visibility** tick **Show the New label** to show the New label, and **Show on the home page** to feature the item. Choose **Published** or **Hidden** under **Status**.
6. Click **Save**. A green message says "Saved." with a link to see it on the website.

> The website does not count stock. It only shows the **In stock** or **Sold out** label you set.

## mark-sold-out | Marking an item sold out or back in stock | roles: owner, support | pages: A10, A11 | group: Merchandise
Quickly change what customers can order.

1. Click **Merchandise**.
2. On the item's card click **Mark as sold out** (or **Mark as in stock**). The label changes at once.
3. To change only one size or colour, click **Edit** and untick **In stock** next to that option, then **Save**.

> Customers cannot add sold-out items to their bag. Orders already received are not affected.
>
> If you marked a whole item as sold out, bring it back with **Mark as in stock** on its card.

## remove-merchandise | Removing a merchandise item | roles: owner, support | pages: A11 | group: Merchandise
Delete an item from the shop.

1. Open the item and scroll to the **Remove** section at the bottom.
2. Click **Remove this item**, then **Yes, remove** in the box that asks "Are you sure? This cannot be undone."

> Old orders still show the item. To hide it for a while, choose **Hidden** instead.

## manage-portfolio | Managing the portfolio | roles: owner, support | pages: A12, A13 | group: Portfolio
Add, edit, reorder or remove photos in the showcase.

### Add a photo
1. Click **Portfolio**, then **Add a photo**.
2. Click **Choose a photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB). A small preview appears.
3. Type a **Picture description** (say what the picture shows, in a sentence), then a **Caption**, and choose a **Category**.
4. Tick **Show on the home page** for your best work.
5. Click **Save**. You return to the **Portfolio** page.

### Edit or reorder
1. On the **Portfolio** page click a photo's caption or its **Edit** button, change the details and click **Save**.
2. To reorder, use the **Move up** and **Move down** buttons on a photo's card. The card shows its position.
3. To feature a photo quickly, tick or untick **Show on the home page** on its card. It saves at once.

### Remove a photo
1. Open the photo and scroll to the **Remove** section. Click **Remove this photo**.
2. Click **Yes, remove** when asked.

> The picture description is required. It helps blind visitors and search engines.
>
> The categories in the list come from **Settings**, under **Category lists**.

## block-dates | Blocking and unblocking dates and times | roles: owner, support | pages: A14 | group: Calendar
Stop customers choosing days or hours when you are not available.

1. Click **Calendar**. Use the arrow buttons to change month. A key under the calendar explains the markings: blocked or partly blocked days, confirmed sessions and waiting requests.
2. Click a day. A panel opens next to the calendar showing what is on that day.
3. To block the whole day, click **Block this whole day**, check the **Date**, add a **Reason**, and click **Block**.
4. To block part of the day, click **Block certain hours**, choose **From** and **To**, and click **Block**.
5. To block several days (for a holiday), click **Block several days** under the calendar, choose **From date** and **To date**, and click **Block**.
6. To open a time again, click the day, then **Unblock** next to the block. A box asks "Unblock this time?". Click **Yes, unblock**.

> Customers cannot choose blocked days or hours on the booking page.
>
> Blocking does not cancel sessions that are already confirmed. If there are any, a yellow message lists them so you can contact those customers.

## answer-enquiry | Answering a message from the Contact page | roles: owner, support | pages: A15 | group: Messages
Read a question and reply.

1. Click **Messages**. The list opens on **Waiting**. Use **Answered** or **All** to see others.
2. Click a message to open it and read it.
3. Click **Reply by email**, or **Message on WhatsApp** if the customer left a phone number.
4. After you reply, click **Mark as answered**.

> Customers expect a reply by email or WhatsApp. If they ask about a booking, point them to the booking page.

## site-settings | Updating contact details, opening hours and delivery areas | roles: owner, support | pages: A16 | group: Settings
Fill in the details marked TBD and keep the rest up to date.

1. Click **Settings**. Each group has its own **Save** button.
2. **Contact and social**: update **Phone**, **WhatsApp number**, **Email**, **Address** and the **Instagram link**, **TikTok link** and **Facebook link**. Anything left empty is hidden from visitors.
3. **Opening hours**: tick the days, set **Opens** and **Closes**, and click **Add hours** for another row. These hours also decide which times customers can book. Click **Take out this row** to remove one.
4. **Booking rules**: set the **Standard session length (minutes)**, the **Minimum notice (hours)** and **How far ahead customers can book (days)**.
5. **Delivery areas and fees**: click **Add an area**, type the **Area name** and **Fee (RwF)**, and keep **Delivers here** ticked. To stop delivering somewhere, untick it, or click **Remove** and confirm.
6. **Category lists**: click **Add a category** to add a portfolio category, or **Remove** to take one away.
7. Click **Save** in that group. A green "Saved" message appears.

> If you leave a delivery fee empty, customers see "to be confirmed" and you type the fee when you approve their order.
>
> Removing a delivery area or category only takes effect when you click **Save** in that group.

## add-staff | Adding a staff member | roles: owner | pages: A17, A18 | group: Team
Give a team member access.

1. Click **Staff accounts**, then **Add a staff member**.
2. Type their **Name** and **Email**.
3. Click **Send invitation**. A panel headed **Invitation link** appears. It says: "Send this link to (their name) by WhatsApp or in person. It works once and expires in 72 hours."
4. Click **Copy link** and send the link to them yourself, for example in a WhatsApp message. The link is also shown in a read-only box.
5. If email is set up for the shop, the panel also says "We have also emailed it."
6. The staff member opens the link, types a password in **New password** and **Repeat the new password** (at least 12 characters) and clicks **Save password**. Then they sign in.

On the **Staff accounts** list a person who has not yet chosen a password shows **Invitation sent**. After that they show **Active**.

> The link cannot be shown again after you leave the page. If you lose it, open the person in **Staff accounts** and click **Get a new sign-in link**. See [Sign-in links, switching off and removing staff](help:manage-staff).
>
> Staff accounts are support staff. They can manage bookings, orders, services, merchandise, portfolio, calendar, messages and settings. They cannot manage staff or edit policies.

## manage-staff | Sign-in links, switching off or removing a staff member | roles: owner | pages: A19 | group: Team
Look after an existing staff account.

### Get a new sign-in link (reset a password)
1. Open **Staff accounts** and click the person.
2. Click **Get a new sign-in link**. A panel headed **Sign-in link** appears with a read-only link and a **Copy link** button.
3. Click **Copy link** and send it to them by WhatsApp or in person.
4. They open the link and choose a new password.

> The link works once. It lasts 72 hours if the person has never chosen a password, otherwise 60 minutes. Any earlier link you made for them stops working.
>
> The link cannot be shown again after you leave the page. Just click **Get a new sign-in link** again to make another.

### If you, the owner, forgot your own password
1. Click **Forgotten your password?** on the **Sign in** page. If email is set up, a link arrives in your inbox.
2. If email is not set up, no link arrives. Ask the person who looks after the website's server to make you a new sign-in link. They do this with a short step on the server itself, and give you the link.
3. Open the link, choose a new password and click **Save password**.

### Switch off access
1. Open the person and click **Switch off access**. They are signed out at once and cannot sign in.
2. To allow them again, click **Switch on access**.

### Remove a staff member
1. Click **Remove this staff member**.
2. Click **Yes, remove** in the box that asks "Are you sure? This cannot be undone."

> Their past actions stay in the records under their name. You cannot switch off or remove your own account, or the owner account.

## edit-policies | Editing policies and the customer agreements | roles: owner | pages: A20 | group: Team
Change the legal texts and the agreements customers accept when they order.

1. Click **Policies and agreements**. Each document shows its version, when it was last changed and a status badge: **Draft – needs review** or **Reviewed**. Documents that are still drafts must be checked by you before the website goes live.
2. Click a document. At the top you see its status again, and a **Mark as reviewed** button if it is still a draft.
3. Edit the **Text**. Use **Key points (one per line)** for the short points shown above the agreement at checkout.
4. Write a short note in **What changed**.
5. Click **Publish as new version**. A box asks "Publish as a new version?". Click **Yes, publish**.
6. When you are happy with a draft, click **Mark as reviewed**. This does not change the text.
7. Under **Past versions** click **View** to read an earlier version.

> Customers must accept the newest version when they book or order. Earlier acceptances stay on record with the exact version each customer agreed to. You cannot change or delete a past acceptance.
>
> The refund policy states that there are no refunds. Please have a professional read all texts before launch.
